Notes & caveats

Each pin shows an establishment's most recent inspection outcome on record; statuses change as re-inspections happen, so confirm current status on the official DineSafe site before relying on a pin. Closed means the establishment was ordered closed at the time of inspection; it may have since reopened. Read the inspection history for recorded infractions and follow the official source for current details. Locations come from the dataset's own coordinates where present, otherwise geocoded from the address, so some pins are approximate.
