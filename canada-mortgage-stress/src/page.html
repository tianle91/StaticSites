Definitions & data sources

“Arrears” and “delinquency” both mean 90+ days overdue here. Quarterly and monthly bank figures remain separate. The monthly price line breaks at the change in TRREB report coverage around 2024; annual history also has changing coverage. Straight lines connect published observations without inventing intervening data. Rising arrears alone do not establish a house-price bottom.
