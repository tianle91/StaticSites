Hover or tap the plot, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to inspect published observations.

Prices cover the Toronto area, not all of Ontario. They are nominal sale averages, not a quality-adjusted HPI. The index compares relative changes since the first shared month. For example, 120 means 20% above that month’s value. The tooltip also shows the original percentage and price.

Definitions & data sources

“Arrears” and “delinquency” both mean 90+ days overdue here. The monthly price line breaks at the change in TRREB report coverage around 2024. The plot is limited to the period covered by both series, so the bank forecast stops at the latest available price month. Straight lines connect published observations without inventing intervening data. Rising arrears alone do not establish a house-price bottom.
