How to read this map

Main-feed rows represent offences and/or victims. One incident can have several rows. “Incidents” counts distinct TPS event IDs within the selected dataset.

Layers overlap: a shooting may also appear as an assault or homicide. Their totals should not be added. These are police-reported records and may be revised; they do not measure all crime or personal risk.

Main-feed inclusion uses report year. Homicide and shooting inclusion uses occurrence year. Switching to occurrence dates cannot recover older incidents reported outside the snapshot's scope. Dates are displayed in Toronto time.

Records without valid Toronto coordinates remain in totals and neighbourhood summaries. Published displacement can affect geographic counts. The summary uses TPS's supplied neighbourhood labels.
